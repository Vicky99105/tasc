JobMatchAI Pattern
Link once, score deterministically, LLM explains but code decides.
  • The Scale Math: Without taxonomy, every role re-run requires 1,200 LLM calls for 10 roles ($3.60+ per run). Re-steering costs money every time.
  • Taxonomy Approach: Link each candidate once (~119 calls), compile rubric once (~10 calls). Matching 1,190 pairs executes at 0 LLM calls in 18ms.
  • Instant Free Re-scoring: When a recruiter amends rubric weights, re-ranking recomputes in memory for $0.00.
  • Full Auditability: Every score points directly to CSV field (skills, headline, certs, projects) and source phrase.

Pipeline Engineering & Data Flow (P1 – P6)
1m 15s
P1 · Data Ingestion & Deterministic Normalization
  • Structured Entity Model: Raw CSVs are ingested into 5 relational dataclasses: Candidate, Role, PastRole, Education, and SkillRaw.
  • Deterministic Normalization Pipeline:
    • Notice Period Normalization: Raw text is parsed into integer days and scored for availability:
      "Immediate / available now" → 0 days (1.00 availability score)
      "1 month notice" → 30 days (0.50 availability score)
      "2+ months notice" → 60+ days (0.00 availability score)
    • Location & Jurisdiction Mapping: Canonicalizes city strings against role office requirements:
      Exact City Match (e.g. Dubai candidate for Dubai role) → 1.00 location score
      Cross-GCC Relocation (e.g. Abu Dhabi candidate for Dubai role) → 0.70 location score
      Outside GCC / Non-Relocating → 0.00 location score
    • Tenure Discrepancy Resolution: Self-reported years contradicted employment dates on 75 of 114 profiles. The system ignores self-reported numbers and computes exact date durations against a pinned reference date:
      Actual Relevant Years = ∑ (Past Role Duration in Years × Occupation Relevance Factor)
  • Deduplication: 120 rows → 119 (dropped 1 blank ID) → 114 distinct usable candidates.
Dual-stream system architecture: candidate normalization & entity linking meeting role rubric compilation
P2 · Dual-Pillar Taxonomy & Graph Topology

  • Strict Foreign Key Isolation: No cross-pillar edges. A DevOps Engineer job title never implies Docker skill directly. Titles count towards relevant years, never verified capability.

P3 · Two-Stage Entity Linking & Field Strengths
  • Stage 1 · Lexical Candidate Proposing: Trigram character indexing and normalized surface matching scans candidate resume text across all fields and proposes top-3 matching taxonomy concepts.
    • How it works: A phrase like "Postgres 14 performance tuning" in projects is normalized to postgres-14-performance-tuning. The trigram index matches the surface "postgres" → surfaces candidates POSTGRESQL (1.0 direct) and SQL (1.0 narrower closure).
    • Occupation surface matching: A past role title "Senior Talent Acquisition Lead" matches TECHNICAL_RECRUITER with a 0.7× partial credit relevance.
  • Stage 2 · Enum-Constrained LLM Disambiguation: When an ambiguous mention has multiple potential meanings, the surrounding sentence is passed to an LLM whose schema is strictly locked to valid taxonomy enum IDs. The model cannot hallucinate or invent new terms.
  • Field Strength Discounts: Evidence is weighted by where it appears on the resume:

P4 & P5 · Deterministic Match Engine & Complete Scoring Walkthrough
  • Exact Skill Matching Formula: For each requirement $R$, the score is the maximum product of graph relation weight and field evidence strength across all candidate mentions:
    Requirement Score(R) = max [ Graph Relation Weight(Term, R) × Field Strength Multiplier(Field) ]
    • Direct skill mention: 1.0 (Direct) × 1.00 (Skills) = 1.00
    • Narrower term match: 1.0 (PostgreSQL for SQL) × 1.00 (Skills) = 1.00
    • Adjacent skill match: 0.6 (Docker for Kubernetes) × 1.00 (Skills) = 0.60
    • Tool in Certifications: 1.0 (AWS) × 0.75 (Certifications) = 0.75
    • Anti-Double Dipping Guard: If a candidate possesses direct evidence for a skill, an adjacent related tool cannot add duplicate points to the same requirement.
  • 4-Column Weighted Scoring Formula:
    Total Score = (Required Mean × 0.80) + (Preferred Mean × 0.10) + (Location × 0.05) + (Availability × 0.05)
  • Experience Band Scoring Table:
    Experience StateConditionScoreBusiness Rationale
    In-Band FitYears ∈ [Min, Max]1.00Target seniority match
    OverqualifiedYears > Max0.8515% discount for potential flight risk / salary mismatch
    UnderqualifiedYears < MinYears / MinLinear proportional penalty (e.g. 1.5y for 3y min = 0.50)
  • Implicit Baseline Skill Demotion: Standard job descriptions often list soft/table-stakes skills (written communication, content creation, data visualization, troubleshooting). These are automatically demoted from Required to Preferred at compile time, preventing senior candidates with specialized tools from failing solely due to resume keyword omissions.
  • Why the Cutoff Threshold is 56.0 (Derived, not Arbitrary):
    • The "2-of-3 Core Skills + Experience" Mathematical Boundary: For a role with 3 required skills, a candidate with 2 direct skills (1.0 + 1.0) and relevant experience (0.85× overqualified or 1.0× in-band) contributes (2.85 / 4) × 80 = 57.0 points before location/availability bonuses. Setting the bar at 56.0 ensures any candidate holding at least 2 core competencies + proven experience advances to recruiter review.
    • Bimodal Distribution Separation: In the 1,200-pair score distribution, cross-department noise concentrates below 42.0, while qualified candidates score ≥ 56.0. The 56.0 cutoff sits in the natural distribution valley, preventing false positives while capturing strong near-misses.

Agent Architecture & Control Flow
40s
Why a Guarded Workflow Instead of an Unconstrained Chatbot?
  • Fixed Business Sequence: Recruiter workflow is inherently linear: Pick Role → Agree on Rubric → Match & Review Shortlist → Approve Summary → Slack Dispatch. An unconstrained chatbot adds prompt complexity and nondeterminism without product benefit.
  • Controlled Execution with Zero Guessing: The agent operates strictly over deterministic underlying tools. LLM routing happens only at Human-in-the-Loop decision gates via intent classification (approve, amend, ask, reject).
  • Closed-World Corpus Isolation: The agent cannot hallucinate new candidates or roles on the fly. Adding roles requires offline taxonomy validation.
  • Free Re-scoring Loop: Amending rubric weights loops back to compile_rubric → run_match ($0.00 compute), enabling infinite recruiter steering with zero token cost.

Limitations & Production Scale Evaluation
25s
Honest Limitations & Trade-Offs
  • Evidence-Only Strictness: Requires stated resume evidence; slightly favours candidates with comprehensive CV descriptions.
  • Placeholder Depth: Thin profiles prevent differentiating 1 year vs 5 years depth on a single bullet.
  • 3-Point Score Band: Scores within 3 points represent close ties rather than strict qualitative rankings.
Inferences Rejected on 119 Rows
  • Skill Co-occurrence: Overfit to 30 synthetic template combinations instead of true labor market patterns.
  • Occupation Priors: Erroneously gave Sales Reps credit for Arabic fluency without explicit evidence.
  • Production Scale Evaluation: Monitored in production via Recruiter Override Rate (frequency of recruiters amending rubrics or rejecting candidates).
